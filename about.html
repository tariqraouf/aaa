We Are Apples Against Apartheid.

A collective of current and former Apple employees bringing attention to Apple's complicity in Israel's apartheid system.

We create campaigns designed to bring attention to Apple's anti-Palestinian racism and Islamophobia. We also work with Friends of Congo, Team Congo, and other Congolese activists to expose how abusive and opaque mineral supply chains harm Congolese communities — and to demand transparency, accountability, and responsible sourcing that benefits Congo's people.

This year, we are calling on communities all over the world to picket their local Apple Stores on the next iPhone launch day. It's time to let the new CEO, John Ternus, know that the world has had enough of companies prioritizing profits over people.
