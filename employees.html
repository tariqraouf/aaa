In Their Own Words

#AppleTruths

Apple likes to portray an image of total perfection. Its employees know better.

The reality is Apple has lost its soul. Tim Cook may have succeeded in making the company more money, but it came at the expense of basic human decency and empathy. Too many times has this company disregarded the human beings that make up its soul.

Apple employees all over the world, especially at retail locations, have reported racism, Islamophobia, and harassment for showing support for Palestine. It's not a singular case, but a repeated pattern of systemic racism from a company that claims to want to fight that very thing.

A couple of years ago, we asked employees to submit anonymous testimonies of what they experienced at work. Here are some of them, word for word.
