Why we're picketing

Show up.
Fight back.

People over profits. Congo and Palestine.

Since 2012, Apple has embraced Netanyahu's apartheid state of Israel and Zionism, choosing to ignore human rights violations recognized by Human Rights Watch and Amnesty International — even after Israel's genocidal attacks on Gaza began. They have also blatantly ignored the Congolese community, who have been those most affected by the demand for the products Apple shamelessly hungers for.

For years, employees have pleaded with Apple executives for empathy, to no avail. The company has reprimanded, suspended, and fired employees who support Palestine and speak out against Zionism. Meanwhile, it has let its own Zionist employees go on racist, Islamophobic rampages with impunity. And the closer Apple has become to Trump, bending the knee to fascism, the more we see how necessary it is to speak up against this normalization of racism and hatred.

On October 23, the launch day for iPhone Duo, we call on communities everywhere to picket their local Apple Stores. We'll provide the flyers, we'll tell you what you need to know. Just show up and help us educate the world, because this injustice deserves to be known. Show up to fight back against Big Tech. Show up to support people over profits. Show up to fight for Congo and Palestine.

Our Demands.

Our campaign demands for Say No to Blood Tech.

Demand 01

Research, Report, Remediate

Apple's business practices have harmed marginalized communities all over the world. If they truly want to “make the world a better place,” they will conduct research on the harm done against these communities, report their findings publicly, and create a plan to meaningfully remediate that harm.

Demand 02

Listen to the Community

We call on Apple to meet with members of the Palestinian and Congolese communities and develop a long-term consultation process to determine corporate policies and activities in the DRC, Israel and Palestine.

Demand 03

Get Out of the Occupation

Apple lists offices in occupied Jerusalem as being in Israel, clearly against international law. They must close these offices and also commit to stopping the inclusion of organizations that fund and arm illegal settlements through their Benevity platforms.

Demand 04

Clean the Supply Chain, Don't Abandon Congo

Apple must prevent minerals controlled, taxed, or trafficked by armed groups from entering its supply chain. It should publish meaningful information about mineral origins, processors, and smelters; strengthen due diligence in conflict-affected regions; remediate identified harms; and support responsible sourcing from legitimate Congolese producers. Responsible sourcing should benefit Congo's people, not exclude Congo from global markets.
